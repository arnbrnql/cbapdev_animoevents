About AnimoEvents

A centralized digital bulletin board and personal calendar for discovering, bookmarking, and joining campus activities across the DLSU Manila and Laguna campuses.

Why we built it

With many student organizations, research centers, and university offices operating across the DLSU Manila and Laguna campuses, announcements are often spread across social media pages, chat groups, and Help Desk Announcement (HDA) emails. It is easy to miss an opportunity or lose the registration details in the middle of another conversation.

AnimoEvents brings those details into one place:

  • Targeted Campus Feed: Organizers can publish university-wide events or restrict visibility to specific roles, student academic levels (Senior High School, Undergraduate, Graduate), colleges, departments/strands, and campuses.
  • Direct Registration & Host Updates: Students and faculty can RSVP directly on an event post, view available slot capacity, and receive official host updates in the event discussion thread.
  • Saved Bookmarks & Personal Calendar: Users can bookmark events with optional personal notes and track all of their registered or hosted activities on a multi-year monthly calendar.
